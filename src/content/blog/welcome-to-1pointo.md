**1pointo** is the operations surface for small businesses that want one place to run compliance, security, and platform work — without hiring an enterprise team to get there.

## What 1pointo is

- **1pointo** is the site and system. It lives at `1pointo.com`.
- **Ahron Darnell** is the person behind it — 20+ years across healthcare, banking, MSP, and enterprise operations, now focused on making that work calmer and clearer for small businesses.

## What you'll find here

- **Guides** on SOC 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA, and secure AI adoption — plain-English, practical, privacy-first.
- **Updates** on what I've built, fixed, or learned — shipped as versioned content so you can see what's new.
- **Community notes** — moderated contributions that earn their way in, not auto-published.

## How content gets published (so you can trust it)

New posts and community contributions move through the **local substrate** — the same orchestration layer that runs this workspace:

1. **Queue** — draft or community submission lands in a local inbox (no direct publish).
2. **Validate** — schema, links, and build checks run locally.
3. **Moderate** — a human approves what ships; automation never publishes on its own.
4. **Build & deploy** — approved content is built as static HTML and deployed to Cloudflare Pages.

That means every post you read here was explicitly approved — not auto-generated into production.
